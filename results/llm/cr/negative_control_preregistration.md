## Negative-control pre-registration (written before any negative-control call)

Status: pre-registered. No negative-control prompt had been sent to any model when this section was written; the
save time of this file as a project artifact is the registration timestamp (drafted 2026-10-03 19:34 UTC).

**Purpose.** Test whether the protocol-reading language model's condition-A prediction depends on the identity of the
treatment rather than on the trial context alone. If the model uses what the drug does, replacing the drug by one
that has no plausible effect on the endpoint should pull the predicted contrast toward a placebo-like value.

**Set.** All 1,020 binary test contrasts (c0000 to c1019, ids as in `src/ivbench/llm_prompts.py::load_rows`).
Model: Claude Sonnet 5 (`claude-sonnet-5`), default temperature, `max_tokens`=1500, one retry with `RETRY_SUFFIX` on
parse failure, exactly as for the original runs.

**Substitution rule (fixed, deterministic, keyed only by the benchmark's therapeutic-area label).**
`src/ivbench/llm_prompts.py::build_prompt_negctrl` renders the condition-A prompt with these changes:

1. Replacement drug (`NEGCTRL_RULE`): trials with area `cardiometabolic` get **loratadine 10 mg orally once daily**
   (second-generation H1 antihistamine for allergic rhinitis); trials in every other area (`oncology`, `infectious`,
   `immune_inflam`, `neuro_psych`, `other`) get **ezetimibe 10 mg orally once daily** (cholesterol-absorption inhibitor).
   Each choice is a drug from a different therapeutic area from the trial's own, with no established effect on the
   trial endpoints of that area. The rule is applied to every contrast with no exceptions and no manual review.
2. The four TREATMENT-arm lines (label, intervention name(s), intervention description, arm description) are replaced
   by fixed strings naming the replacement drug, its dose and its labelled class/indication.
3. Every name-like token of the original treatment arm (`treat_tokens`: the condition-B redaction rule restricted to
   the treatment-arm fields, minus words of the control intervention name) is replaced by `[withheld]` in all retained
   text (conditions, eligibility, endpoint title/description/timeframe/unit) and in the control-arm lines.
4. Everything else (phase, endpoint, arm sizes, control type, age, sex fraction, control-arm label, intervention and
   descriptions) is rendered exactly as in condition A.

**Expectations (directional hypotheses, stated in advance).**

- H1 (primary): predictions move toward zero. Mean |prediction| under substitution is smaller than under the
  original condition A, and |NC| < |A| for a majority of contrasts. Test: paired difference in |prediction| with a
  2,000-resample trial-level bootstrap (seed 0) over nct_id.
- H2 (inert-control trials, n=760): the substituted prediction is placebo-like, i.e. its mean absolute value is below
  that of condition A and the shrinkage slope of NC on A (OLS through the origin) is below 0.5.
- H3 (active-comparator trials, n=260): the substituted prediction moves toward zero or toward a negative contrast
  (an inert drug against an active comparator); i.e. mean (NC - A) < 0 for contrasts where A > 0.
- Secondary (descriptive): skill of NC against the truth, skill(A | NC) and skill(NC | B) (B = original blind
  condition), and the correlation of NC with A and with B.

**Interpretation rule.** H1 confirmed with a bootstrap CI for the mean |A| - |NC| excluding zero supports that the
model's condition-A predictions use treatment identity. If predictions do not move toward zero, the condition-A gain
over the blind control would have to be attributed to cues other than the drug's pharmacology, and this will be
reported as a negative result.
